Aster Stock Desk

A walkable paper-trading floor for Aster DEX stock perpetuals (NVDA, TSLA, MU, QQQ…), running GMS PMR v1.4.

  • W A S D or arrows walk · drag to look · wheel steps · Shift runs
  • 1 Screener · 2 Trader · 3 Reviewer · 4 Market Intel · ⚙ Settings
  • B movers board (north) · P positions (east) · V review wall (south) · R rules + comms (west)
  • Click a desk, a wall or a trade card to open it · Esc closes · H toggles this help

Paper only. No orders reach Aster. Click anywhere to start.
